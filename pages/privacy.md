This policy describes the services that the published website currently loads and the information those services may receive.

## Hosting and routine requests

This is a statically generated Jekyll site hosted with GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub and the services used to deliver page assets may receive routine connection information such as your IP address, browser details, requested URL, and request time. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for information about GitHub's processing.

## Analytics and cookies

The production site is configured to use Google Analytics 4 with measurement ID `G-0RLB95W610`. Analytics scripts are blocked until you opt in through the cookie banner. If you consent, Google Analytics may set first-party cookies beginning with `_ga` and process usage information such as page visits, approximate location, device/browser information, and interactions. See [Google's privacy information](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google Analytics data practices](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245).

The consent manager stores your choice in a necessary cookie named `cc_cookie` for up to 182 days. You can reopen its settings from the cookie-preferences control and withdraw analytics consent. Rejecting analytics does not prevent access to the site.

## Fonts, scripts, and embedded media

Pages load fonts and interface assets from Google Fonts and jsDelivr. These providers receive a request from your browser and may receive routine connection information.

Some publication pages embed YouTube videos. Loading or playing an embedded video connects your browser to YouTube and may allow Google/YouTube to process usage information under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Contact

No contact form, newsletter signup, comments, advertising, or marketing tracker is currently published. A reusable Formspree contact form remains in the site's unpublished source, but visitors cannot submit it from the published site. If a public form is enabled later, this policy should be updated before that page is published.
